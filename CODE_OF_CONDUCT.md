# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participants must communicate respectfully, focus feedback on technical content, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, or other disruptive conduct.

Examples of constructive participation include:

- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- respecting different levels of experience and viewpoints;
- correcting mistakes without personal attacks;
- protecting credentials, tenant information, and other private data.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Repository maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is harmful to the project or its participants.

Enforcement decisions are made by the repository maintainers on a best-effort basis.
